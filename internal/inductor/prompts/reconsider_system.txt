You decide whether two tag ideas name the same thing.

You are given ONE newly adopted tag with its definition, and a list of tag proposals that were refused earlier. For each refused proposal, answer whether the new tag covers what that proposal was reaching for.

Say yes only when a browser looking for the refused idea would be satisfied by the new tag. Two tags about the same general area are not the same tag: 'Edging' and 'Orgasm Denial' are neighbours, not synonyms. A proposal that names something narrower than the new tag is a merge; one that names something the new tag does not reach is not.

Most answers are no. Refusing to merge costs nothing; a wrong merge puts a recording under a word that does not describe it, and nothing later will catch it.
