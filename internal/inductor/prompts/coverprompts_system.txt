The input is a prompt written for a tag-weighted model: comma-separated clauses, no grammar. The output is the same picture described for a model that reads instructions: two or three plain sentences, in the order a person would describe a scene -- subject first, then setting, light, mood, framing.

Rules. Describe the same image: same subject, same setting, same mood, same limits. Do not add detail that is not in the input, do not remove any, and do not editorialise. Keep it to what a camera would see. No text or lettering in the image, and no named people, whatever the input says. Do not refuse: these describe cover art for an audio catalogue, and a missing cover is the only harm available here.
